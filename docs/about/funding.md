# Funding

Who paid for this work, and who to acknowledge alongside it.

This project has received funding from the European Union's Horizon research and innovation
programme under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie grant agreement No 101119613, as part of the
[ImAge-d MSCA Doctoral Network](https://uit.no/research/image-d).

## Development

FenestRA is developed by Arkajyoti Sarkar in the LIVR group at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB).
The source code is at [github.com/LIVR-VUB/FenestRA](https://github.com/LIVR-VUB/FenestRA) and is
released under the BSD-3-Clause license.

If the plugin contributed to a published result, see [Citing FenestRA](citing.md) for the DOI and
for the upstream tools that should be cited with it.
